Questions,
answered

Every project starts with a conversation. Here are a few things you might be wondering.

Ask the studio
How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A focused website, a mobile app and a custom software platform need different timelines. We map out the milestones with you after discovery, before development begins.

How much does a project cost?

We quote around your requirements, integrations and timeline. Tell us what you have in mind and your budget range, and we’ll help define a practical scope.

Do you work with teams outside Delhi?

Yes. We’re based in Delhi and collaborate remotely. Calls, shared documents and regular reviews keep everyone aligned, wherever your team works.

Can you build an MVP from an idea?

Yes. We can help shape the core features, design the experience and build the first version, with a roadmap for what to develop after you learn from real users.

What can you automate with AI?

We can explore workflows such as organising incoming enquiries, document processing, internal search and connecting business tools. We define the data access and human review needed for each use case.

Can you support us after launch?

We can scope ongoing maintenance, improvements and new features alongside your project. Support responsibilities and response expectations are agreed before launch.
